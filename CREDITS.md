## Books

Downloaded from [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org) (CC0), [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org),
[Pro Git](https://git-scm.com/book) (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0), [Eloquent JavaScript](https://eloquentjavascript.net) (CC BY-NC 3.0)
and [SICP, unofficial EPUB](https://github.com/sarabander/sicp-epub) (CC BY-SA 4.0).

The Sport & Health shelf adds public-domain scans from the [Internet Archive](https://archive.org)
(Hackenschmidt, Kellogg, Liederman, Macfadden, Müller, Sandow, Saxon) and open textbooks from the
[eCampusOntario Open Library](https://openlibrary.ecampusontario.ca) (Athletic Therapy Skills CC BY 4.0,
Human Anatomy Lab Manual CC BY 4.0, Human Anatomy and Physiology CC BY-NC-SA 4.0,
The Art & Science of Personal Wellness CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) and
[University of Hawaiʻi Pressbooks](https://pressbooks.oer.hawaii.edu) (Human Nutrition 2e, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).

## Sleep screens (`assets/screensavers`)

Reproductions of public-domain works: Albrecht Durer (Melencolia I, Knight Death and the Devil, Rhinoceros),
Gustave Dore (Inferno, The Raven), Ernst Haeckel (Kunstformen der Natur), Utagawa Hiroshige (Sudden Shower),
Rembrandt (Self-portrait), Aubrey Beardsley (Volpone), John James Audubon (Bird of Washington).
Converted to 1236x1648 grayscale.
