## Sleep screens (`assets/screensavers`)

Reproductions of public-domain works: Albrecht Durer (Melencolia I, Knight Death and the Devil, Rhinoceros),
Gustave Dore (Inferno, The Raven), Ernst Haeckel (Kunstformen der Natur), Utagawa Hiroshige (Sudden Shower),
Rembrandt (Self-portrait), Aubrey Beardsley (Volpone), John James Audubon (Bird of Washington).
Converted to 1236x1648 grayscale.
